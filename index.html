01 / About Me

A little about me.

I’m Shahan Ahmed (শাহান আহমদ), a Bangladeshi student and digital content creator originally from Zakiganj, Sylhet, and currently based in Doha, Qatar. I enjoy creating videos and reels that combine creative thinking, humour, motivational messages and everyday lifestyle. Through my personal brand, “This Is Your Shahan,” I’m building a consistent and authentic online identity.

My goal is to keep learning, create meaningful content and connect with people through my work.
